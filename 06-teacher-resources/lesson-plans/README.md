# Lesson Plans

*Kilele TechPath — Teacher Resources*

## Lesson plan template

Copy this template for each lesson and store completed plans in this folder with a descriptive filename, e.g. `grade-8-python-input-lesson.md`.

```markdown
# Lesson title

- **Grade:**
- **Learning area / module:**
- **Duration:**
- **Prerequisites:**

## Learning objectives
By the end of the lesson, learners should be able to...

## Required materials
- Devices / software
- Printed resources
- Safety requirements

## Key concepts
- Concept 1
- Concept 2

## Lesson procedure
1. Introduction and motivation (10 min)
2. Learning objectives
3. Concept explanation (15 min)
4. Demonstration (15 min)
5. Guided practical activity (25 min)
6. Independent challenge (15 min)
7. Discussion and reflection (10 min)
8. Assessment
9. Assignment or extension task

## Practical activity
Task, instructions, success criteria and evidence to collect.

## Assessment
Method, rubric link and competency levels.

## Extension activity
For learners who finish early.

## Homework
Optional follow-up task.

## Teacher reflection
What worked? What will you change next time?
```

## Suggested 90-minute allocation

| Activity | Time |
|---|---:|
| Introduction | 10 minutes |
| Concept explanation | 15 minutes |
| Demonstration | 15 minutes |
| Guided practical | 25 minutes |
| Independent challenge | 15 minutes |
| Review and reflection | 10 minutes |

Teachers may adapt the duration to the school timetable and available resources.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Objectives are observable and age-appropriate.
- [ ] At least one hands-on activity is included.
- [ ] Safety, privacy and ethics are addressed where relevant.
- [ ] Alternative offline/low-device activity is prepared.
- [ ] Assessment evidence is identified.
- [ ] Reflection prompt is included.
