# Teaching Guides

*Kilele TechPath — Teacher Resources*

## Teaching approach

Teachers should combine explanation, demonstration, guided practice, independent work and reflection.

A suggested lesson structure:

1. Introduction and motivation
2. Learning objectives
3. Concept explanation
4. Demonstration
5. Guided practical activity
6. Independent challenge
7. Discussion and reflection
8. Assessment
9. Assignment or extension task

## Teacher responsibilities

Teachers should:

- Prepare lessons and test activities in advance.
- Explain learning outcomes clearly.
- Demonstrate procedures.
- Support practical learning and monitor progress.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Assess practical competence.
- Encourage curiosity and collaboration.
- Promote safe and responsible technology use.
- Maintain learner progress records.
- Support different learning speeds.

## Teaching documentation requirements

Each lesson should have:

- Lesson title
- Grade
- Learning area
- Duration
- Prerequisites
- Learning objectives
- Required materials
- Key concepts
- Lesson procedure
- Practical activity
- Assessment
- Extension activity
- Homework
- Teacher reflection

## Teacher preparation

Teachers should be familiar with the tools they intend to introduce. They should test practical activities before classroom delivery and prepare alternative activities for situations involving limited connectivity or devices.

## Inclusion and accessibility guidance

- Plan for shared devices and rotational group work.
- Prepare offline materials and locally installed software.
- Provide guided exercises, extra practice and extension challenges.
- Offer alternative ways for learners to demonstrate competence.
- Consider readable text, clear instructions, keyboard accessibility, screen reader compatibility, color contrast, captions and assistive technologies.

## Safety guidance

- Integrate digital safety throughout every grade.
- Cybersecurity activities must use teacher-approved, simulated or explicitly authorized environments. Never test real systems without permission.
- Electronics work must use safe, low-voltage educational equipment and supervision.
- Verify age requirements, account policies, privacy settings, licensing and institutional approval before learners use online services.
