# Foundation Module — Artificial Intelligence

*Revisited across Grades 6–11 with increasing complexity*

## Core concepts

- AI applications
- Machine learning concepts
- Training data
- Generative AI
- Prompt literacy
- AI limitations
- Bias
- Privacy
- Responsible use and human oversight

## Expected competency

A learner can explain introductory AI concepts and evaluate AI-generated outputs critically.

## Grade progression

| Grade | Focus |
|---|---|
| 6 | Awareness of smart devices and automation around us |
| 7 | Responsible online participation; evaluating online information (foundation for AI literacy) |
| 8 | Meaning of AI; examples; machine learning awareness; data and AI; AI limitations; bias; responsible use |
| 9 | AI applications; prompt writing; AI-assisted productivity; accuracy; verified research tasks |
| 10 | Training/testing data; classification and prediction; model evaluation awareness; generative AI; bias |
| 11 | Data preparation; model evaluation; NLP/generative AI; prompt engineering; AI ethics and oversight |

## Responsible AI reminders

- AI can make mistakes — verify its outputs.
- AI systems may reflect bias.
- Do not share personal information unnecessarily with AI tools.
- AI supports, rather than replaces, independent thinking.
- Maintain academic integrity.

## Suggested practical activities

- Compare human-generated and AI-generated explanations and evaluate accuracy.
- Use an approved AI tool for a supervised research task, then verify its output.
- Explore a beginner-friendly classification demonstration with a safe educational dataset.
- Compare outputs from different prompts and discuss bias and limitations.

## Mastery evidence

Learners explain how AI systems learn from data, write effective prompts, critically evaluate outputs and use AI responsibly.
