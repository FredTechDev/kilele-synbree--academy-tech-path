# Foundation Module — Digital Citizenship

*Revisited across Grades 6–11 with increasing complexity*

## Core concepts

- Privacy
- Security
- Digital identity
- Digital footprints
- Copyright
- Responsible communication
- Cyberbullying prevention
- Digital wellbeing

## Expected competency

A learner can participate responsibly and safely in digital environments.

## Grade progression

| Grade | Focus |
|---|---|
| 6 | Personal information; passwords; suspicious links; online strangers; cyberbullying; asking trusted adults |
| 7 | Digital identity; privacy; password hygiene; copyright; plagiarism; digital wellbeing; classroom citizenship guide |
| 8 | AI bias and responsible use; safe online creativity; attribution |
| 9 | Multi-factor authentication; phishing; social engineering; data protection; backups |
| 10 | Security principles; access control; data ethics; responsible innovation |
| 11 | Privacy and ethics in capstone work; professional communication; ethical data handling |

## Safety reminders

- Personal information should not be shared unnecessarily, including with AI tools.
- Access to a system does not grant permission to test or modify it.
- Cybersecurity practice must use teacher-approved, simulated or explicitly authorized environments.
- AI-generated information requires verification and academic integrity must be maintained.

## Suggested practical activities

- Identify safe and unsafe online scenarios.
- Create a digital safety awareness poster.
- Analyze simulated phishing messages.
- Develop a classroom digital citizenship guide.

## Mastery evidence

Learners protect accounts, recognize threats, credit sources and communicate respectfully online.
