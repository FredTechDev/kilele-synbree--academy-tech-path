# Vision, Mission and Philosophy

*Kilele TechPath — Programme Framework*

## Vision

To develop digitally capable, innovative and technologically confident learners who can understand, create and responsibly use technology to solve real-world problems.

## Mission

To provide structured, practical and accessible technology education that prepares learners for further learning and participation in an increasingly digital world.

## Core philosophy

Every learner should have an opportunity to **discover technology before being expected to specialize in it**.

The programme recognizes that learners possess different interests, abilities and learning preferences.

- Some learners may enjoy programming.
- Others may demonstrate stronger interests in design, data, robotics, networking, cybersecurity or digital media.

The programme must provide meaningful exposure to these different disciplines.

## Guiding principles

1. Learning by doing.
2. Understanding before memorization.
3. Problem-solving before tool dependency.
4. Creativity alongside technical knowledge.
5. Responsible and ethical technology use.
6. Collaboration and communication.
7. Progressive learning complexity.
8. Practical evidence of competence.
9. Equal opportunity for technology discovery.
10. Continuous exploration and improvement.

## Programme objectives

By completing the programme, learners should be able to:

1. Explain fundamental computing concepts.
2. Identify and describe computer hardware and software.
3. Operate common digital devices confidently.
4. Manage files, folders and digital information.
5. Use productivity applications.
6. Navigate the internet responsibly.
7. Evaluate the reliability of digital information.
8. Understand digital citizenship and online safety.
9. Apply computational thinking to practical problems.
10. Develop algorithms and flowcharts.
11. Understand fundamental programming concepts.
12. Create simple software applications.
13. Develop basic web pages.
14. Understand databases and structured information.
15. Interpret and communicate data.
16. Explain basic networking concepts.
17. Understand introductory electronics and robotics.
18. Explain artificial intelligence at an introductory level.
19. Recognize cybersecurity risks and protective practices.
20. Collaborate on technology projects.
21. Use version control at an appropriate level.
22. Document and present digital projects.
23. Explore technology career pathways.
24. Identify personal interests and strengths.
25. Develop a practical technology portfolio.
