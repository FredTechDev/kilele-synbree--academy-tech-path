# Pathway H — Multimedia and Creative Technology

**Primary interest:** Creating digital media and experiences

*Grade 11 primary specialization pathway · Kilele TechPath*

## Overview

Learners in this pathway produce digital media — graphics, audio, video, animation and interactive stories — for real communication purposes.

## Topics

- Graphic design
- Photography
- Image editing
- Audio production
- Video editing
- Animation
- Motion graphics
- Digital storytelling
- 3D design awareness
- Interactive media
- Copyright
- Creative project management

## Suggested technologies

GIMP · Inkscape · Blender · Audacity · Kdenlive · Other suitable creative tools

## Capstone examples

- Educational animation
- Digital awareness campaign
- Interactive storytelling project
- Multimedia learning resource

## Grade 10 exploration

Before specialization, learners complete one digital storytelling project as part of pathway exploration.

## Creative ethics

Respect copyright, attribute sources, use licensed or self-created media, and represent people and communities respectfully.
